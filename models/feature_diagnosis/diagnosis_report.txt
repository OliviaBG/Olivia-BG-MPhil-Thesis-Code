Cross-check: point-biserial correlation (linear, not just monotonic)
------------------------------------------------------------
AUC only assumes a MONOTONIC feature-label relationship (robust to step
functions like flank_hpr_likelihood's NESmapper likelihood-ratio bins).
Point-biserial correlation is literally Pearson r against the binary label,
so it assumes a LINEAR one. Where the two agree, that's extra confidence;
where they disagree, the feature's relationship to the label is likely real
but non-linear rather than absent.
